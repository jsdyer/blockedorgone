    Reading the evidence

    These measurements reflect the networks that ran tests. Counts can rise when testing activity increases.

    No anomaly
    OONI found no sign of interference in that measurement.
    Anomaly
    A result differed from expected connectivity. Routine network errors and outages can produce similar results.
    Confirmed by OONI
    A measurement matched an OONI censorship fingerprint, such as a known block page or DNS response.
    Failed test or no measurements
    A failed test could not complete. A coverage gap means the archive returned no tests for that day.
